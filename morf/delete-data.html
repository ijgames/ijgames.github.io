Delete your Morf data

Morf by ij games

How to ask

  1. Email jon.daveyan@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Morf data".
  2. Send it from the Google account you use for Play Games, or write your Play Games name in the email.
  3. I delete your data within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your Play Games player ID and Play Games name
  • Your saved game progress (levels played and beaten, attempts, Rhombs, skins and unlocked levels)
  • Levels you published
  • Your invites (who accepted your invites and whose you accepted)
  • Your reports of levels
  • The scrambled (hashed) record of your Morf Pro purchase

What is kept

  • Anonymous gameplay analytics. They are tied to a random install ID, not to your account, so they can't be found from your name or ID.
  • Total play and win counts on levels, which don't record who played.
  • Your Morf Pro purchase itself, which Google Play handles. Deleting your data doesn't refund it.

Data on your phone is removed when you uninstall Morf.
