Morf Publishing Terms

Last updated 30 October 2026

These terms apply when you publish a level you made in Morf's level builder. By ticking the box and tapping Publish, you agree to them.

Publishing is public and permanent

A published level can be played by every Morf player. Once published, you can't change it or take it down. To make a new version, copy the level and publish the copy.

Your name

Your level is shown with your Google Play Games name, so other players see who made it.

What you let us do

You keep ownership of your level. You give ij games a free, worldwide, permanent permission to host, show, copy and distribute it in Morf and to promote Morf with it. This includes offering it as part of Morf Pro, which players pay for. You don't receive payment for published levels.

What you may publish

Only levels you made yourself. Level names must not be offensive, hateful, sexual, threatening or misleading, and must not contain personal information or advertising.

Removal

ij games may hide or remove any published level, at any time, for any reason, including reports from other players. We may also stop a player from publishing.

Changes

We may update these terms. The date at the top shows the latest version. The terms that applied when you published a level keep applying to it, except where the law requires otherwise.
